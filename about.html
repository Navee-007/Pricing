Profile

Professional Story

I am a Full Stack Developer with 3 years and 7 months of hands-on experience designing, building and deploying software that businesses run on every day. Based in Coimbatore, I work primarily in the MERN stack, with SQL Server for relational workloads and React Native for mobile.

The systems I have shipped are the unglamorous kind that have to be right — a banking portal where a stale balance is a real problem, a POS counter that cannot oversell stock, an accounting ledger that has to reconcile. My focus is on getting the data model and the business rules correct first, then building an interface clean enough that people actually want to use it.

I work directly with clients, not through a layer of account managers. You talk to the person writing the code, which means scope questions get answered the same day instead of the same week.

"Technology should support human work seamlessly. My goal is to turn business complexity into applications that are fast, modular and maintainable."
